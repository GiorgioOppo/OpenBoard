# Code of conduct

OpenBoard welcomes contributors and users of different backgrounds and experience levels. Participation in project issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces requires respectful, constructive behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with evidence; disagree without personal attacks.
- Be patient with questions, feedback and differences in experience or language.
- Give appropriate credit and respect privacy and confidential information.
- Accept reasonable moderation and help keep discussions focused on the project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized comments, deliberate disruption, impersonation and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For problematic public content, use GitHub's **Report content** menu. For conduct that violates GitHub's platform rules, use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). A project moderation request can also be opened through [support](SUPPORT.md), identifying the relevant public discussion without repeating abusive text or publishing private information. Do not disclose personal details in a public report.

The [maintainer](MAINTAINERS.md) may clarify expectations, edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, issue a warning, or restrict participation when needed. The response depends on severity, context and repeated behavior. A participant may request reconsideration through the same support process. GitHub handles platform-level abuse reports under its own policies.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate [private vulnerability-reporting channel](SECURITY.md#report-a-vulnerability).
